About Bitvulex

A software company built on doing fewer things, properly.

We build four things — websites, apps, integrations and POS software — and we build all four the same way: scoped clearly, priced honestly, delivered on what was promised.

Why Bitvulex exists

Too many software projects fail not because of bad code, but because of vague scope, shifting requirements and promises that were never realistic to begin with. Bitvulex was started to fix that — every engagement begins with a clearly defined package, so both sides know exactly what's being built, for how much, and by when.

How we're structured

Rather than keeping a large in-house bench, Bitvulex runs lean: a project manager scopes and oversees each engagement, a business development lead manages client relationships, and a digital presence lead keeps our own brand and yours visible. Specialist developers are brought onto each project based on what it actually needs — so you're paying for the right skill set, not a fixed overhead.

What "integrity" means here

  • We quote fixed packages wherever the scope allows it — no open-ended hourly surprises.
  • We tell you when something can't be done in your budget, instead of over-promising and under-delivering.
  • We hand over what you need to run your own product — accounts, source files, documentation.
  • We stay reachable through the support period included in your package.

Where we're headed

We're growing service by service — starting with web, app, API & integrations and POS software — and adding new capabilities only once we can deliver them to the same standard.
