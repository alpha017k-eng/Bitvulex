    Site Settings

    Site theme

    Sets the default look for every visitor. Visitors can still flip the toggle in the header for their own view, but new/first-time visitors always see this.

    Contact inbox

    The email address shown on the Contact page and used as the mail recipient.

    Automatic email delivery (EmailJS)

    Optional. Without this, submitted messages are saved here and open a pre-filled email draft for the visitor to send. With it, messages (including package inquiries) are emailed to you automatically at your notification email — set that under My Account. Create a free account at emailjs.com, then paste your IDs below.

    🔐 Two-factor login (email verification code)

    Adds a one-time 6-digit code as a second login step, sent to each account's own email — even if someone gets a password, they can't get in without the inbox too. Uses the same EmailJS Service ID and Public key entered above, in a second template. Create it the same way (Code view → paste admin/email-otp-template.html), then paste its Template ID below.

    Users & Roles

    Administrator only. Create accounts, change anyone's role, username, email or password, and remove access. Node accounts can only reach Services & Packages, Demo Projects, Messages, Social Links, Growth Stats, SEO Assistant and Site Settings — Team, Users & Roles and My Account are administrator-only.
