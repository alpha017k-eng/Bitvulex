Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Bitvulex collects through this website and how it's used.

1. Information we collect

When you use the contact form on this website, we collect the information you choose to submit: your name, email address, phone number (if provided), the service you're enquiring about, and your project message.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To recommend a suitable package or provide a custom quote.
  • To keep a record of prior communication if you become a client.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. How your message reaches us

Submissions from the contact form are delivered to the email address configured by Bitvulex's admin team, and a copy is kept for our internal reference. We do not use your contact details for marketing unless you separately opt in.

4. Cookies & local storage

This website does not use tracking cookies. Some pages use your browser's local storage purely to display up-to-date content (such as current packages and team members) and, on the admin panel, to manage editable content — this data stays on the device used to access the site and is not transmitted to third parties.

5. Data retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as necessary to respond to you and, if you become a client, for the duration of our working relationship plus a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping.

6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Contact us using the details on our Contact page to make a request.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or practices change. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to us via the Contact page.
